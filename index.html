The Hurdle, online

Two players, one secret word, six guesses.

How to play
One player picks a 5-letter word. The other has 6 guesses to find it. After each round you swap roles.
GREEN right letter, right place.
ORANGE the letter is in the word, in a different place.
PURPLE the letter is in the word, but you have already found every copy of it.
RED the letter is not in the word.
The Hints button reveals a random letter from the word.
